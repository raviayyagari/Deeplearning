What the agent does with your brief

It works through the same steps a good analyst would, calling a research tool at each one and showing you what it found.

1

Reads your brief

Idea, campus or city, customers, price, budget and any interviews.

2

Explores competitors

Who sells what nearby, at what visible price, with get_competitors.

3

Maps customer needs

Ranks needs that today's options don't meet well.

4

Builds the business model

Segments, value proposition, channels, revenue and costs on one canvas.

5

Estimates the economics

Setup and running costs, margin, break-even and 36 months of cash.

6

Checks assumptions

Flags what's unrealistic and tests what a 20% miss would do to profit.

7

Lists what to validate

Questions, experiments, success signals and an interview guide.
